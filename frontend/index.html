How this simulator calculates risk

Maximum borrow = collateral value × 75%. Health factor = (collateral value × 80%) ÷ debt. A health factor above 1.0 is above the simplified liquidation threshold; at or below 1.0, the position is considered at risk. This is an educational model, not a complete DeFi protocol.
